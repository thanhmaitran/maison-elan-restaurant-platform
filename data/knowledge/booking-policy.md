# Booking policy

## Reservations
Demo policy: reservations are confirmed only after the server has checked availability and returned a confirmed status. Please arrive near the reserved time and contact the restaurant if plans change. The live restaurant must replace this text with its actual policy.

## Booking rules
Tables are held for 105 minutes (135 minutes for parties of 6 or more). Bookings can be made up to 60 days ahead and at least 2 hours before the time on the same day. Online bookings are for up to 8 guests.

## Cancellation
Demo policy: customers may request cancellation through their account. Whether a booking is cancelled immediately or requires staff approval is determined by the implemented business rules. Replace this sample policy before production. Signed-in guests can cancel from the account page.
