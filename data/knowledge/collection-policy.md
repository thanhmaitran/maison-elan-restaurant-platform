# Ordering and payment policy

## Ordering
Demo policy: menu prices and availability are rechecked by the server before an order is accepted. An order is not confirmed merely because the customer clicked the button; confirmation follows a successful backend response.

## Dine-in and collection
Guests can order to their table by choosing a table number at checkout, or order for collection. Staff confirm each order before the kitchen starts. No delivery service is offered.

## Payment
Guests can pay at the table, on collection, or online by card or QR code when online payment is enabled.

## Allergens
Demo notice: ingredient and allergen records are illustrative software data and are not suitable for real food service. In a real restaurant, declarations must be checked against exact recipes, supplier specifications, kitchen processes and cross-contact controls. Guests with allergies should contact the restaurant directly.
